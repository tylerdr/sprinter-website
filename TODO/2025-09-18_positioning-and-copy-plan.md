# 2025-09-18 Positioning & Copy Plan

## Context
Sprinter must communicate a portfolio-wide AI transformation story for lower-mid and mid-market private equity (PE)—with people-first adoption, document intelligence, and automation as core pillars of that broader mission. This plan covers every copy and narrative deliverable across the marketing site, ensuring messaging consistency with the Website Vision Constants and competitive insights.

## Objectives
- Lock a cohesive narrative that reinforces Sprinter as the AI Operating Partner for LMM/MM PE portfolios.
- Clarify packages vs. services while keeping pricing transparent and outcome-driven.
- Produce persuasive, defensible copy for case studies, industries, and governance content that resonates with PE buyers and frontline operators.
- Embed the AI transformation arc across all surface areas, weaving in the People → Process → Projects → Product philosophy and document intelligence as signature pillars.

## Status (2025-09-18 Audit)
- No checklist items completed yet; hero, packages, and industry copy still reflect legacy positioning.
- Pricing tiers and guarantees remain mismatched with docs (`/ai-sprint` still advertises $50k offer).
- Dependencies: need approved messaging guide and case study source data before drafting.

## Detailed Plan
### 1. Master Messaging Guide
- Document hero statement, sub-head, proof bar metrics, and CTA language.
- List copy anchors to repeat ("Start small. Win early. Expand.", etc.).
- Outline audience personas (OP, CFO/COO, Portfolio CEO) with outcome bullets.
- Include narrative for document intelligence differentiation vs. competitors.

### 2. Package & Pricing Narrative
- For each package, specify target buyer, scope, inclusions/exclusions, SLAs, and guarantee language.
- Provide FAQ answers (desktop ERP support, change management, governance deliverables).
- Prepare pricing comparison table and toggle copy (monthly vs. per sprint).

### 3. Services Library
- Create modular write-ups (TL;DR, outcomes, scope bullets, timeline, acceptance criteria, governance notes).
- Ensure document intelligence services remain primary; tie each to relevant wedge examples and KPIs.

### 4. Industry Playbooks
- For each priority industry: articulate top document types, frontline pain, expected gains, and relevant case study teasers.
- Emphasize people-first adoption stories (e.g., "dispatchers got lunch breaks back").

### 5. Case Studies
- Capture before/after metrics (touchless rate, cycle time, exception reduction, work NPS).
- Highlight human adoption outcomes along with financial impact.
- Include governance artifacts delivered (policy pack, audit logs).

### 6. Governance & Compliance Story
- Explain Responsible AI framework components (policy, HITL thresholds, audit trails, safe middle layer).
- Provide clarity on data residency, security, SOC2 alignment, and regulatory references (GDPR, HIPAA as applicable).

### 7. Approach Pillar
- Expand on People → Process → Projects → Product seq.
- Turn the "lunch break" and "AI done for me" narratives into polished copy.
- Provide checklists and acceptance criteria for each stage.

### 8. Newsletter & GTM Content
- Title: "AI for PE"; articulate promise, topics, cadence, and lead magnet (100-day AI Playbook for OPs).
- Draft registration microcopy, welcome email outline, and drip summary.

### 9. AI Opportunity Atlas Content Templates
- Author intros and TL;DR blocks for each entity template.
- Provide sample KPIs, acceptance criteria, and governance notes to seed writer workflows.

### 10. Interactive Tool Microcopy
- Write prompts, input labels, CTA text, result summaries, gating language.
- Ensure messaging reinforces empowerment, adoption, and wedge framing.

### 11. Reviews & Approvals
- Run copy through readability checks (aim for Flesch 50–60 where possible).
- Gather feedback from sales, delivery, legal, and design.
- Capture final approval timestamps in shared tracker.

## Testing Criteria
- [ ] Content proofread for grammar, clarity, and consistency by two reviewers.
- [ ] Messaging validated with at least two target personas (sales/customer interviews or internal SMEs).
- [ ] Legal/compliance review completed for guarantees, claims, and security statements.
- [ ] All metrics in case studies traced to source data; governance sign-off recorded.
- [ ] Copy integrated into design mocks passes accessibility contrast for text placement.

Deliverables from this plan unblock IA, design, and development workstreams.
