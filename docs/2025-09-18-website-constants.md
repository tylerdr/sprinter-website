# 2025-09-18 Website Vision Constants

These principles are treated as stable guardrails for all future work on the Sprinter marketing site. They originate from leadership direction captured on 2025-09-18 and should be referenced before proposing structural, content, or UX changes.

## AI Transformation North Star
- Sprinter leads with an **AI transformation mission** for LMM/MM PE portfolios—combining strategy, document intelligence, workflow automation, training, and governance into a single operating system.
- People-first adoption, document intelligence, and end-to-end automation are **pillars** of that transformation, not the entire story. Copy must reinforce the broader transformation arc (vision → roadmap → pilots → scaled operations).
- Messaging should surface how Sprinter orchestrates AI portfolio-wide: opportunity mapping, wedge sprints, operating partner retainer, transformation squads, and education/governance programs working together.

## Audience & Positioning
- Sprinter presents itself as the **AI Operating Partner for lower-mid market (LMM) and mid-market (MM) private equity funds** and their portfolio companies.
- Primary buyers: Operating Partners, CFOs/COOs, and Portfolio CEOs who need measurable EBITDA impact, defensible governance, and transparent pricing.
- Messaging must spotlight the full AI transformation lifecycle (strategy, document intelligence, process automation, agentic tooling, governance) with document intelligence as a signature wedge but not the sole focus.
- Every offer reinforces that Sprinter delivers **30–45 day wins** with guarantees that are defensible to investment committees and LPs.

## Philosophy: People → Process → Projects → Product
- Technology is not the main obstacle—**adoption is**. Every initiative starts by earning trust with the people who will live with the workflow.
- Narrative shift requirement: move from "AI done to me" to "AI done for me" by highlighting how AI removes drudgery, returns lunch breaks, and lets teams focus on high-value work.
- Engagements always start with a **specific, high-love-use-case wedge** that benefits frontline users, then scale only after value is proven and adoption sticks.
- Sequencing doctrine:
  1. **People** – Co-design with the humans in the loop; measure "work NPS" alongside financial KPIs.
  2. **Process** – Map the real workflow, capture desired future state, bake in governance and compliance.
  3. **Projects** – Ship tightly scoped two-week sprints with human-in-the-loop guardrails and clear acceptance criteria.
  4. **Product** – Graduate successful wedges into durable automations or internal products when stakeholders pull for scale.

## Commercial Structure Non-Negotiables
- Maintain **four primary packages with transparent pricing**:
  - AI Operating Partner Retainer (fund-level AI PMO & governance)
  - Transformation Partner (outcome-based squads on sprint cadence)
  - AI Sprint (two-week wedge to prove value)
  - Education & Enablement (workshops, bootcamps, governance training)
- Packages stay simple for buying; granular services (discovery workshops, agent development, integration, training) live under a Services hub.
- Guarantees: missed acceptance criteria trigger Sprinter-funded remediation; portfolio governance artifacts are always included.

## Experience & Content Tenets
- Site must act as an **AI Opportunity Atlas**: interlinked entities covering use cases, pain points, goals, technologies, industries, concepts, products, and ideas across the full transformation lifecycle.
- Highlight transformational journeys: vision workshops, wedge sprints, scaled automation, training, and governance—showing the arc from first win to portfolio-wide change.
- Interactive tools (roulette, calculators, wedge planners) are mandatory on key pages, either ungated or softly gated for lead capture.
- Every page reiterates the core phrases: "Start small. Win early. Expand." and "AI done for people, not to people." while reinforcing AI transformation as the umbrella narrative.
- Case studies cover document intelligence, workflow automation, agentic copilots, and change management outcomes with anonymized but descriptive labels if NDAs block logos.
- Governance, responsible AI, and safe middle-layer narratives appear wherever integrations with desktop ERPs/LOS/legacy systems are discussed.

Keep this document updated when leadership revises positioning or core philosophy.
