PE AI Readiness Checklist

Assess Your Portfolio's AI Transformation Potential

Transform Your Portfolio with AI

This comprehensive checklist helps PE firms evaluate AI readiness across their portfolio companies. Use it to identify quick wins, prioritize investments, and build your AI transformation roadmap.

1 Data & Infrastructure Readiness

Centralized Data Platform
Data is consolidated in a cloud data warehouse or lake (e.g., Snowflake, Databricks)
Data Quality Standards
Established data governance with quality checks and master data management
API Infrastructure
Modern APIs for system integration and real-time data access
Cloud Adoption
Core systems migrated to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
Security & Compliance
Data security protocols and compliance frameworks in place (SOC2, GDPR)

2 Organizational Readiness

Executive Sponsorship
C-suite actively champions AI initiatives with clear vision and budget
AI/Data Team
Dedicated data scientists, ML engineers, or AI product managers on staff
Change Management
Proven track record of successful technology adoption and change initiatives
Training Programs
AI literacy and upskilling programs for employees
Innovation Culture
Culture that encourages experimentation and data-driven decision making

3 Use Case Identification

Process Automation Opportunities
Identified repetitive, manual processes suitable for AI automation
Customer Experience Enhancement
Clear opportunities for AI-powered personalization and service improvement
Revenue Generation
AI use cases that directly drive new revenue or increase pricing power
Risk Management
Applications for fraud detection, compliance, or predictive maintenance
Competitive Differentiation
AI capabilities that create sustainable competitive advantages

4 Financial & Performance Metrics

AI Budget Allocation
Dedicated budget for AI initiatives (typically 5-10% of IT spend)
ROI Framework
Clear methodology for measuring AI investment returns
KPI Tracking
Established KPIs for AI initiatives with regular monitoring
Quick Win Potential
Identified AI projects with <6 month payback period
Scale Potential
AI solutions that can scale across portfolio companies

5 Current AI Maturity

Basic Analytics
Using BI tools and dashboards for reporting and analysis
Predictive Analytics
Leveraging ML for forecasting and predictive insights
Process Automation
RPA or intelligent automation deployed in production
AI Products/Features
AI-powered features integrated into products or services
GenAI Adoption
Using GPT, Claude, or other GenAI tools in operations
